Three rules to build against

Roles attach to a project, not a person. Barry is 1st AD on this job and a director on the next one. If you store the role on the user record you will rebuild this in three months.

Every write is staged, then published. A half-typed schedule reaching 76 phones is the failure that ends the pilot. Draft state also gives the producer a moment to override before it goes out.

Nothing is ever hard deleted. Call times are evidence in a rate or overtime dispute. Every change keeps who, what, and when — that log is a reason for a production company to keep paying you long after the shoot wraps.
