Turnover

The call sheet stops being a PDF

A live, personalised call sheet for commercial film production. Every person on the sheet opens one link and sees only their own day. Production sees who has actually read it, and can move the schedule without sending another attachment.

02 · THE PROBLEM

Three documentsthat stopped agreeing

Not a hypothesis. What one ordinary shoot day looked like — a well-run job, an experienced team, and paperwork doing what paperwork does.

3ATTACHMENTS, ONE EMAIL
Call sheet, shooting schedule and production booklet, sent together to roughly thirty people. Each restates the same call times, so each is another chance to disagree with the others.
17:37THE NIGHT BEFORE
Sent the evening before a 07h00 unit call. Anyone asleep, driving or out of data finds out at the gate — and there is no way to know who has read it.
3CONTRADICTIONS, ONE DAY
Lunch appears as both 15h00 and 15h15. The minimum temperature is given as 2°, 3° and 4°. A gear check is dated twice. Nobody made a mistake — one file was updated and the others weren't.
0RECORD OF CHANGES
When the day slips, the schedule moves in a WhatsApp group half the crew have muted. Nothing survives to settle an overtime dispute three weeks later.

04 · PERMISSIONS

One app.The role decides what's in it.

Not four products. One build, where the tabs, the editable rows and the visible data all change according to who production invited and as what.

COORDINATOR

Builds and publishes

Uploads the sheet she already makes. Sees who has confirmed, nudges the gaps, tracks minor hours and catering counts.

1st AD

Runs the day

The only role that moves a time or wraps a shot. Changes stage as a draft and go out in one publish.

CREW & CAST

Read, confirm, sign out

Their own call, their own wrap. Their department plus every head of department. Three actions, no more.

AGENCY

Progress only

A guest seat on one project. No contact details, no cast personal data, no other jobs on the slate.

Two things sit outside the model on purpose. Reporting a safety concern is available to every role including guests, and crew can reach each other directly — a grip needing the gaffer shouldn't have to go through production. See the full permission matrix →

06 · HOW IT GETS BUILT

Ship narrow,then widen

Each phase starts only once the previous one has run on a real shoot.

Phase 1 · Links and receipts

The sheet is entered by hand the night before.

  • One link per person, no install
  • WhatsApp delivery
  • Opened / confirmed tracking

Phase 2 · Coordinator takes over

Only once crew open the link unprompted.

  • Import from the existing template
  • Crew directory across jobs
  • Roles, draft-and-publish, change log

Phase 3 · The live day

Built last. A delay push is noise until the link is trusted.

  • Shift a block, notify only the affected
  • Minor hours, sign-out timesheets
  • Read-only agency view
